# Balsam Fir

*Abies balsamea*



![Abies balsamea tree with cones, Sherburne National Wildlife Refuge, Minnesota](img/balsam-fir-1.jpg)

![Abies balsamea 2](img/balsam-fir-2.jpg)

Abies balsamea or balsam fir is a North American fir, native to most of eastern and central Canada (Newfoundland west to central Alberta) and the northeastern United States (Minnesota east to Maine, and south in the Appalachian Mountains to West Virginia).

## Quick Facts

| | |
|---|---|
| **Scientific name** | *Abies balsamea* |
| **Family** | Pinaceae |
| **Height** | 45-75 ft |
| **Bloom time** | Cones, not flowers |
| **Sun** | Part shade |
| **Moisture** | Mesic to wet |
| **Soil** | Acidic loam, peat |
| **Wildlife value** | Winter cover and browse; seed for crossbills and red squirrels |
